Example: morning check

Goal. Remind operators every weekday morning to do a daily check.

Rule.

AT 08:00 on Mo Tu We Th Fr
DO
  Send notification
    to: Operators   via: Push   message: Morning access-control check started

Steps.

  1. Create a scenario and drag AT into the workspace.
  2. Leave the time at 08:00. Several times can be typed separated by commas, for example 08:00, 14:00.
  3. Make sure Mo to Fr are ticked and Sa and Su are not. This is the default.
  4. Drag Send notification into DO. Pick the team, choose Push and type the message.
  5. Save and enable.

Result. The notification goes out at 08:00 on weekdays.
