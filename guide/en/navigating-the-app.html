Navigating the application

The top bar

From left to right:

  • Logo - click it to open or close the left navigation menu.
  • Breadcrumbs - show where you are and let you step back up. On narrow screens they are shortened.
  • Search box - see Searching and filtering.
  • Your name and avatar - click to open the user panel (shows your name and Logout).
  • Incident indicator - appears only while there are active incidents, with a count. Click it to open the incident panel. See Incidents.

The left navigation menu

The menu lists the areas you may use. A typical menu, in order:

Items appear only if your account may open them and, for some areas, your licence includes them. Additional pages supplied by installed adapters can also appear in the menu.

Under Maintenance & Settings you find General (language), Card Formats and Holiday Calendars.

At the bottom of the menu is a Commands area with the access synchronization command, which sends current access rights to the devices. While it runs, the button shows its progress in percent.

Pages with tabs

Detail pages (for example a person, a team or a device) show tabs such as settings, teams, access and audit. Click a tab to switch.
